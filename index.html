The UL1741-SB gap.

02 · Cert posture

This is the single most actionable finding for Lisa. SRNE has a real cert posture for the older revision of UL1741, plus a cETL mark — fine for off-grid, fine for back-up, fine for places that don't connect to the utility. For a paid, AHJ-inspected grid-tied install in North Carolina, the AHJ is increasingly going to ask for UL1741-SB (the IEEE 1547-2018-aligned revision) and the SRNE current US line does not publicly document it.

Cert · audit
SRNE has UL1741 + cETL. It does not have UL1741-SB.
What's there

UL1741 + CSA-C22.2 No. 107.1 + cETL

Documented on SRNE's official blog and on the HEBP-Pro / SEI-12K-UPRO product datasheets. Covers off-grid, back-up, self-consumption, and most permitted residential installs where grid-ride-through compliance is not the binding constraint. This is real cert and Borick / Signature Solar have shipped these into US installs that pass inspection at the AHJ level.

What's missing

UL1741-SB · IEEE 1547-2018

The revision that codifies grid-ride-through behavior, anti-islanding under modern utility profiles, and reactive-power priority. Increasingly required on paid grid-tied residential and all commercial. Not documented on any current SRNE US split-phase model as of this brief. Sol-Ark, EG4 (18kPV), and Solis S6-EH all carry it. For Lisa's commercial work this is the line.
